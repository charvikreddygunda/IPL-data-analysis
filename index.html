The Auction Room

A data science game: you're the new analyst at an IPL franchise.

Unofficial fan-made learning game, not affiliated with the IPL, BCCI or any franchise. Team names and colours are used for reference only; there are no official logos. Vikram (Director of Cricket) and Meera (Head of Analytics) are fictional characters. Player stats are calculated from IPL ball-by-ball data for 2024–2026 (the 2026 season is partial), and current squads come from each player's most recent team. Nationality and wicketkeeper labels were added by hand. The purse and retention slabs follow the 2025 mega-auction rules; squads are capped at 15 for the game. Ground profiles are simplified game assumptions.
